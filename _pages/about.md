# Research Interests
My research interests center on understanding and improving LLMs. During my MPhil study, I focused on optimizing the vanilla GRPO algorithm so that it could be better implemented on small-sized LLMs, which sparked my lasting interest in the reasoning abilities of LLMs and the attention mechanism behind them. I am also keen on the explainability of LLMs — I once visualized the attention score matrices of an LLM during inference and became curious about why they look the way they do and how such insights could benefit the LLM community. More recently, I have been exploring training-free Vision-Language Navigation (VLN) for embodied UAVs, as well as using RL methods to improve the tool-use ability of LLM agents.

- Reasoning with LLMs.
- Attention mechanism in LLMs.
- Explainability of LLMs. 
- Vision-Language Navigation (VLN) for embodied UAVs.
- LLM Agents.
