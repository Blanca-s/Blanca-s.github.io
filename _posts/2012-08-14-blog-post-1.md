Graduation is a big thing. In my impression, most students are about to graduate will take many graduation photos and then post a pyq to congratuate themselves. When I was a student he who just finished his Gaokao, I searched many info online. At that time, Zhihu is a very popular platform where people post their ideas on everything. I was very into those topics like "How does it feel like studying in ×××". So, I talked to myself that when I graduate from my undergraduate school, I shall write a detailed blog on my own university. 

But at that time, I was only 18, there are still 4 years before I graduate from my undergrad school. I didn't know what will life be like. Now, I already graduated from my grad school and I still haven't wrote anything on it. Actually, I was not intend to write anything anymore after those years. But my girlfriend always asked me:" well, when will you post your pyq?" Giving a thought about it, I think I do need to write something about it. 

However, I have too many words to say. If I post such a long passage on my pyq, nobody will give it a shit. To be Honest, if my friend post a long passage, I won't read it eighter. In a Chinese argot “Too long to read". However again, not long ago, I happened to read a blog by [Alisa Liu](https://alisawuffles.github.io/), I was like, well I could write those words on my own website and I only post the weblink of this blog and write a short comment on it. If someone wants to see it, he could read it himself. 

Thus, I decide to write this blog. Dedicated to my life over the last six years. 


High school
======
I have nothing to say about my high school life. Unlike most students in China, I spent a beautiful time when in high school. 

Beautiful but unconscious. What does it feel like. I think it is not knowing what exactly I am doing. Like I was on a train to some place and everyone tells me how fantacy this place is but all I can do is to imagine with my poor social and life experience. Being admitted to an university, getting a good job is the only life style I knew at that time. I did not know what is a university like, I don't what is a good job. Simply few worlds propagate from adults to me, like, "algorithm", "programmer", "Civil Servant". Why can't help me to walk through this? I thought maybe because I was stupid. Later, I understood that this is because they did not know themselves, either. 

Nevertheless, I am still very grateful to my high school. While other schools drove their students hard as hell, my high school always held many events to help us have a colorful campus life. And our teachers won't put any barrier to them, instead, they always take part in those events and play with us. This really helps me to grow into a healthy person. It is when I grow up and look back on the past that I understand that health, both physically and mentally, is much more important to our overall well-being than have a good grade.

Unluckily, I still don't know how exactly we could live on this world but what's better is that I know I don't know right now. I know that the world could be colorful. That is the reason why I never stop stepping forward.
